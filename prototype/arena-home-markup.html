Full grading rules and benchmark designation

These are SPX Arena’s proposed performance tiers, not statistical proof of skill or risk-adjusted grades. The 5Y badge uses the same annualized thresholds. The 1Y season uses Legendary ≥10 pp, Epic ≥4 and <10 pp, Rare ≥0 and <4 pp, and Common <0 pp. A full 10-year record is required for the main rank; 5–9 years qualify only for a separate five-year division.

The S&P 500 card’s Legendary status is a special benchmark designation. It is not an investor rating and has no leaderboard rank. SPX identifies the index; the return figures and comparisons use SPY ETF market-price total returns. SPIVA’s statistics compare active funds with the index itself.

All periods end December 31, 2025. Ten years starts December 31, 2015; five years starts December 31, 2020; one year starts December 31, 2024. Full-career results are separate from the main rank. Rounded source data can leave a badge Unrated when it falls too close to a threshold, as with PSH’s five-year result.
