How this calculation works & data sources

Uses SPY’s dividend- and split-adjusted closing price on the last trading day of each month. The starting investment is valued at the close immediately before your start month. Monthly additions use month-end prices; “beginning” uses the previous month’s closing price. Fractional shares and reinvested distributions are assumed.

Values are historical estimates before personal taxes, trading costs and inflation. ETF expenses are reflected in its prices. This is a historical replay, not a forecast or a guaranteed return. Monthly endpoints do not show fluctuations within each month.
