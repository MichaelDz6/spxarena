## Reuse requirements

[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) permits commercial reuse and adaptations with the required attribution, source/title and license information, disclosure of changes, and no suggestion of endorsement. Place the credit in the card's source details or the site's linked credits page. Retain the original files and evidence when making optimized site copies.

This verifies the stated copyright license in the source records, rather than guaranteeing every possible use is risk-free. The license does not clear separate publicity, privacy or moral rights. The intended use here is identification of the people discussed in the site's case studies.

## Evidence and scope

- `sbf-commons-evidence.json` preserves both Commons API records, revision IDs, source URLs and license metadata, including source-page wikitext.
- `portraits.json` records filenames, dimensions, hashes, credits and per-person status.
- `openverse-search.json` preserves the qualified Openverse searches, filtered for commercial reuse and modification. Narrow queries may miss untagged or differently described photographs.
- Commons exact-name searches, targeted web/Flickr searches and original-source leads were also checked. Search-result labels and site-wide text licenses were not treated as sufficient photo licenses.
- Both verified SBF images are now integrated into the local site: the Cointelegraph portrait on his case card, and both images in the expanded photo credits and `/credits/#arena-of-ruin-portraits`. Optimized WebP copies and their hashes are recorded in `src/data/ruin-portraits.json`.
- No outreach, paid licensing, AI portrait generation or deployment was performed.
