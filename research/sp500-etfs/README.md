# S&P 500 ETF comparison

Issuer materials checked 19 October 2026. Source URLs live with each fund in
`src/data/sp500-etfs.js` and are exposed on the comparison page.

US annual expense ratios: SPYM 0.02%, VOO 0.03%, IVV 0.03%, SPY 0.0945%.
UCITS ongoing charges/TER: SPYL 0.03%; CSPX/SXR8, VUAA and VUSA 0.07%.
UCITS identifiers distinguish share classes; exchange tickers are examples.
Issuer logos reuse the assets and attribution already on the Credits page.

The illustrative annual cost is published fee × 10,000 with a constant balance,
not a forecast or additional broker charge. No return ranking or liquidity
ranking is inferred from the fees. SPYM formerly used SPLG and only began
tracking the S&P 500 in January 2020. Accumulating and distributing share
classes must be compared on a total-return basis. Trading currency does not
imply currency hedging. Availability is not guaranteed for any account.

Refresh issuer documents before changing fees or tickers. Do not automatically
change the verification date without rechecking the materials.
