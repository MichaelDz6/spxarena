# Polish ETF broker additions — checked 19 October 2026

Availability is matched at fund/share-class level; exchange, ticker, currency,
account eligibility and trading permissions must still be checked at the broker.

## mBank

Official indexed instrument list:
https://www.mbank.pl/pdf/ind/inwestycje/lista-akcji-zagranicznych.pdf

The indexed document identifies itself as the 18 November 2025 list; direct
retrieval currently redirects. Search-index excerpts confirm these entries:
- SPYL, LSE USD: IE000XZSV718. The list labels it DIST, but the ISIN matches the
  accumulating share class; do not copy that inconsistent label.
- CSP1, LSE GBP: IE00B5BMR087, the same share class as CSPX/SXR8.
- VUAA, LSE USD: IE00BFMXXD54 (also in the official ETF list).
- VUSA, Xetra EUR: IE00B3XXRP09.
