A vehicle for structured giving: Stanley Druckenmiller establishes his foundation to handle a large sum

In 2008, Stanley Druckenmiller received a payout beyond what he could give away in a year. He and his wife, Fiona, started the Druckenmiller Foundation to provide a convenient structure for making big bets on education, poverty, health, and the environment.
