# Rights audit — October 13, 2026

## Implemented

The current policy is **authentic licensed color photographs only**. There are 19 published photos: 7 original photo files and 12 source-backed AI enhancements/crops, including two publisher-licensed interview poster frames. Fifteen investors have no published photo while reuse permission is unresolved. No generated likenesses or drawings remain in public assets. The previous illustrations are archived privately in `research/retired-ai-portraits/`, not shipped.

All published photos have a matching entry in `src/data/portrait-credits.json` with source, creator, license, modifications, original-input hashes where enhanced, final file hash, actual dimensions and a color flag. All are at least 800 × 800 pixels. Grayscale filters were removed. Enhancement prompts and input roles are recorded in `research/portrait-photo-enhancement-prompts.json`. AI is limited to source-backed photographic enhancement, not invented replacement portraits. It can reconstruct detail, so exact documentary fidelity is not promised.

See `research/PHOTO-CLEARANCE.md` and `research/portrait-clearance-pending.json` for the 15 unresolved investors, rejected matches, and an unsent permission-request draft. No image licenses were purchased and no third-party messages were sent. A publisher's or Commons contributor's license assertion is evidence of permission, not a universal non-infringement guarantee.

Unverified photos and the undocumented illustration sheet were removed from `public/`, including unused files. Previous provenance in `research/portrait-sources.json` is historical evidence only, not the current asset inventory. It must not be used to restore those images without clearance. Only `dist/` should be deployed; research files include third-party source materials and must not be published wholesale.

Profile pages show full credits and link to the actual delivered image file. `/credits/`, linked globally, lists all images, source links, creator credits, license links, changes, and ShareAlike adaptation notices. Company logos, including Micron's unlicensed favicon, were replaced with original CC0 ticker tiles. Independent editorial use and non-endorsement notices were added.

Upstream Astro and D3 license notices ship in `public/third-party-notices.txt` and are regenerated by the build. Google Fonts remain remotely served by Google's CSS service rather than bundled font files.

October 15 update: the local card implementation now includes calculated drawdown summaries in `src/data/card-drawdowns.json`, with explicit estimate, date, frequency and proxy labels. This replaces the previous blanket withholding behavior on cards following the request to use approximate data and weekly observations. Raw daily and weekly histories remain in research and are not shipped. No provider license or redistribution permission has been obtained or inferred from that request; the source-permission questions below remain unresolved. The historical profile-comparison dataset remains empty, and those fixed-period comparisons continue to use disclosed year-end observations. The prior daily dataset remains in `research/sources/drawdowns-unlicensed-review.json`.

## Remaining verification — not cleared by notices

- The SPX Arena name/domain has not received a comprehensive trademark clearance search. Descriptive index references and non-affiliation notices do not guarantee that site branding is legally clear. A rename or counsel's clearance is the next step if the brand will be commercially used.
- Annual return numbers and issuer holdings remain a factual research snapshot with source citations. Copyright generally does not protect facts, but source-provider contracts, acquisition conditions, database rights outside the U.S., and index licensing restrictions can still matter. Permission for Yahoo/FinanceCharts-derived annual NAV/price observations and State Street holdings has not been verified. Source attribution does not resolve that question. Obtain appropriate rights or independently replace those observations from permitted primary disclosures before claiming a completely cleared dataset.
- Biographies are concise factual summaries with source links. No exhaustive source-by-source text similarity audit or independent confirmation of the original site's design provenance has been completed. Retain evidence of original authorship, and do not reuse publisher prose or source report artwork.
- CC licenses do not automatically clear publicity/personality rights, endorsement claims, or all jurisdictions' public-domain rules. The site uses images to identify editorial subjects and explicitly disclaims endorsement. Additional commercial use requires its own assessment.

Published license compliance and withholding unverified images reduce the identified risks; they do not establish a universal non-infringement guarantee. Only cleared photographic inputs are supplied for enhancement.

## Maintaining assets

Do not add a third-party image without documented source, creator, applicable license, license link, and modification notice. Record the checksum, verify the subject's identity, and inspect the downloaded payload. ShareAlike adaptations must retain the applicable license. `npm test` checks the public asset inventory, image decoding, minimum resolution, hashes, original enhancement inputs and prompt provenance against the documented records. `scripts/package-portraits.mjs` only re-encodes approved photographic enhancements and rejects text-generation records; all shipped asset bytes are checked by the tests.
