# Rights audit — October 13, 2026

## Implemented

All 34 investors now have full-resolution raster portraits, with no initials avatars. Each shipped WebP has documented provenance, actual pixel dimensions (at least 800 × 800), file size and a SHA-256 checksum in `src/data/portrait-credits.json`. Eighteen are original AI editorial illustrations generated from text alone, without any third-party photograph or artist-style reference as an input. Sixteen use Commons photographs with documented CC BY, CC BY-SA or U.S. public-domain status; nine of these have been enhanced with AI and cropped for profile use. A Commons license record is evidence of the published license, not a guarantee that every uploader held the necessary rights.

All generative work used the built-in ImageGen tool. The exact final prompts, input roles, dates and tool output paths are archived in `research/portrait-generation-prompts.json`. Licensed inputs used for enhancements are retained in `research/portrait-inputs/` with hashes, and the original source metadata remains in `research/portrait-license-verification.json`. Web encoding uses Sharp without creative changes or rescaling. Original AI output files remain in the default generated-images directory; the final delivered WebP files are saved inside the project under `public/portraits/`.

Generated portraits carry a visible “AI illustration” label on cards and profiles, descriptive alt text, and a usage notice. They are interpretive likenesses, not documentary photographs. No exclusive copyright is asserted in wholly machine-generated elements. Enhanced photographs carry an “AI enhanced” label; photographer attribution and original licenses are retained, and the adaptations are offered under the same source licenses. AI enhancement can reconstruct detail, so exact documentary fidelity is not promised. The published images do not imply endorsement.

Unverified photos and the undocumented illustration sheet were removed from `public/`, including unused files. Previous provenance in `research/portrait-sources.json` is historical evidence only, not the current asset inventory. It must not be used to restore those images without clearance. Only `dist/` should be deployed; research files include third-party source materials and must not be published wholesale.

Profile pages show full credits and link to the actual delivered image file. `/credits/`, linked globally, lists all images, source links, creator credits, license links, changes, and ShareAlike adaptation notices. Company logos, including Micron's unlicensed favicon, were replaced with original CC0 ticker tiles. Independent editorial use and non-endorsement notices were added.

Upstream Astro and D3 license notices ship in `public/third-party-notices.txt` and are regenerated by the build. Google Fonts remain remotely served by Google's CSS service rather than bundled font files.

Daily-price-derived drawdown data has been withheld from the published application pending provider permission verification. The prior dataset is retained as `research/sources/drawdowns-unlicensed-review.json`; the generator now writes a research candidate, not the approved application dataset. All published drawdowns currently use year-end returns and disclose the limitations.

## Remaining verification — not cleared by notices

- The SPX Arena name/domain has not received a comprehensive trademark clearance search. Descriptive index references and non-affiliation notices do not guarantee that site branding is legally clear. A rename or counsel's clearance is the next step if the brand will be commercially used.
- Annual return numbers and issuer holdings remain a factual research snapshot with source citations. Copyright generally does not protect facts, but source-provider contracts, acquisition conditions, database rights outside the U.S., and index licensing restrictions can still matter. Permission for Yahoo/FinanceCharts-derived annual NAV/price observations and State Street holdings has not been verified. Source attribution does not resolve that question. Obtain appropriate rights or independently replace those observations from permitted primary disclosures before claiming a completely cleared dataset.
- Biographies are concise factual summaries with source links. No exhaustive source-by-source text similarity audit or independent confirmation of the original site's design provenance has been completed. Retain evidence of original authorship, and do not reuse publisher prose or source report artwork.
- Neither CC licenses nor AI generation automatically clear publicity/personality rights, endorsement claims, or all jurisdictions' public-domain rules. The site uses images to identify editorial subjects and explicitly disclaims endorsement. Additional commercial use requires its own assessment.

AI generation and published license compliance reduce identified risks; they do not establish a universal non-infringement guarantee. All generated prompts avoid requesting recreation of a particular published image, and no unverified third-party photograph was supplied to ImageGen.

## Maintaining assets

Do not add a third-party image without documented source, creator, applicable license, license link, and modification notice. Record the checksum, verify the subject's identity, and inspect the downloaded payload. ShareAlike adaptations must retain the applicable license. `npm test` checks the public asset inventory, image decoding, minimum resolution, hashes, original enhancement inputs and prompt provenance against the documented records. `scripts/package-portraits.mjs` can re-encode the archived final outputs if their source paths remain available; all shipped asset bytes are checked by the tests.
